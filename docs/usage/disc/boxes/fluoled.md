Features and Applications
Build Your Fluorescence Microscope Enhance your CoreBox setup with LED-based fluorescence imaging. The openUC2 FluoBox LED Edition offers a flexible, user-friendly path to building and customizing your fluorescence microscope, whether for advanced research, teaching labs, or hobby projects.

LED-Based Excitation: Safer and simpler than traditional laser systems, reducing regulatory and handling concerns.
Optimized Beam Shaping: Collimation and focusing lenses enable precise illumination, suitable for high-quality fluorescence imaging.
Dichroic and Emission Filtering: Achieve clear separation of excitation light and emitted fluorescence for efficient detection.
Köhler Illumination Setup: The integrated iris helps with precise alignment, improving brightness uniformity and image contrast.
Infinity Microscopy: Combine with the Infinity Box for professional-grade, infinity-corrected imaging of fluorescent samples.
